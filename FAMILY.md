# How to add the family app source to Feather

You need the **Feather** app installed and signed on your iPhone or iPad.

## The link

```
https://inf-nicolasperez.github.io/family-apps/repo.json
```

(If that does not match what you were given, use the link you were sent instead.)

## Add it

1. Open **Feather**.
2. Tap **Sources** at the bottom.
3. Tap the **+** in the top right.
4. Paste the link above.
5. Tap **Add**.

## Install an app

1. Go to **Sources** and open **Family Apps**.
2. Tap the app you want.
3. Tap **Get**.
4. Feather downloads it, signs it with your Apple ID, and installs it.

New apps and updates show up there automatically — you never have to re-add the link.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Invalid source"** — the link is wrong or your connection is down. Open the link in
  Safari first; you should see a wall of text starting with `{"name":`.
- **Source is empty** — nothing has been published yet. Ask the person who owns the repo.
- **Download fails** — try again on Wi-Fi. Some apps are very large.
- **Installs but crashes when opened** — tell the person who owns the repo; that file
  needs to be replaced, it is not something you can fix on your phone.

## Two things to know

- Every device signs apps with **its own Apple ID**, and a free Apple ID only allows
  3 apps and 7-day certificates. Feather re-signs them when they expire — keep Feather
  installed.
- Do not share this link outside the family.
